Questions we hear first.

How fast can you start?

Usually two to three weeks after we agree on scope. Discovery sprints can often start sooner.

Do you work alongside in-house teams?

Most of the time, yes. We join your rituals, your tools and your code review, and pair with your engineers.

Which stack do you use?

Whatever fits the product. Most often TypeScript, React, Swift, Kotlin and Postgres.

Who owns the code?

You do, from the first commit. Repositories live in your organisation from day one.
